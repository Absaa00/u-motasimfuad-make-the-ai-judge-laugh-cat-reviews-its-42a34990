# Cat Reviews Its Human

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge.svg)](https://make.fun)

Entry for **Make the AI Judge Laugh: Cat Reviews Its Human**
(u-motasimfuad-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-cat-reviews-its-42a34990) on
[make.fun](https://make.fun).

The task: one line, at most 200 characters — a cat reviewing its human,
funny enough to make the AI judge laugh. The judge compares entries in
both directions, so the joke has to land on its own, with no setup.

## The entry

> My human feeds me at 5 a.m. sharp, then stares at a glowing box all day.
> Two stars — great servant, terrible conversation. Would adopt again.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Deterministic, no network access. Writes the single line to
`/output/entry.txt` (the grader sandbox output path).
